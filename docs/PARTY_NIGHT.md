# Running the November 22 party

## Before guests arrive
1. Open the control room for the event on your phone and stay signed in.
2. Tape up the printed QR posters (entrance, bar, DJ booth).
3. Scan one yourself and add 5 songs to warm up the queue.
4. Decide your rules in **Party shuffle settings** (e.g. max songs, clean only).

## During the party
- When the DJ/Offtrack starts a song from the top of the queue, tap **Next song**.
  This is what makes NOW PLAYING and the replay accurate.
- To play a specific request: ⋯ → **Play now**. To bump one: ⋯ → **Play next** or ▲.
- A song didn't fit? **Skip** (it won't appear in the replay). Inappropriate? **Remove**.
- Too many requests? **Pause requests**. Guests can still heart.
- Someone lost their phone or typed the wrong handle? **Guests** tab → Remove / reset.
  They can then join again.

## At the end
1. Tap **End event** (tap twice to confirm). Everyone's screen shows the replay link.
2. Optional, with Apple Music set up: **Apple Music & Offtrack → Create replay
   playlist**, then share its public link in the event details.
3. Share the replay page link (`/e/<code>/replay`) on your Instagram Story using
   the Story image from that page.
